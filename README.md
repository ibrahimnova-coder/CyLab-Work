# CyLab-Work
Hello there! I'm Ibrahim, and this repository is dedicated to showcasing 10 of the challenges I finished during my work with CyLab Security Academy, a free computer science education program created by security experts at Carnegie Mellon University. But they're not just any 10 challenges ; they're the challenges that taught me, interested me, and made me go "you can do that?!" the most. In my opinion, just because a challenge is hard doesn't automatically make it good. The quality, quantity, learning experience, satisfaction and the interest all matter more than the difficulty. And for me, these challenges were precisely that. I learned about so many new things I'd never even known about before - perceptrons, webshells, assembly, debugging, SQL, and the list goes on. So let me share my experiences with you, too!
